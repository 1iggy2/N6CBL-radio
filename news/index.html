Colombian amateurs in earthquake response; keep emergency frequencies clear

ARRL Source ↗ OPS

After the 10 August 2026 earthquake in Colombia, LCRA and club stations have been restoring repeaters and supporting rescue comms. IARU R2 asks stations to stay off 3985, 7060, 7240, 7275, 14300, and 21360 kHz, plus 146.520 MHz in VHF range of Colombia.

FCC cites GMRS licensee for a linked repeater network

ARRL Source ↗ POLICY

Enforcement Bureau issued a Notice of Violation to the licensee of WQYU407 for internet-linking nearly two dozen GMRS repeaters. Linking is legal in amateur radio; the FCC says it is not in GMRS because the service has only eight shared repeater pairs.
