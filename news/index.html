ARISS receives Challenger-7 recognition

ARRL Source ↗ SPACE

Amateur Radio on the International Space Station was named a Challenger-7 honoree as part of the Challenger Center's 40th anniversary. Frank Bauer KA3HDO said the student-ISS contacts carry the same exploration and STEM brief as the STS-51-L crew. Other honorees include AIAA, the American Rocketry Challenge, and Janet's Planet.

Year of the Club: website and newsletter contest winners

ARRL Source ↗ OPS

More than 80 entries in each contest. Website first: Cumberland Valley ARC W3ACH. Newsletter firsts by size: Gloucester County ARC W2MMD, Ozaukee Radio Club W9CQO, and Peekskill/Cortlandt ARA W2NYW. Winners get a nod at Huntsville, 21–23 August, and in QST.

Arizona 10-year-old KM7HFU licensed, given an FT-857D

ARRL Source ↗ OPS

Reynaldo Mangual passed Technician and is now KM7HFU. Maricopa Amateur Radio Association handed him a donated Yaesu FT-857D, LDG YT-100 tuner, and a restored WWII J-38 key. He is MARA's youngest member and also in the Pinal County 4-H club.

SM/DF7CW on Kungsleden through 27 August, spotting via SOTAmāt

QRPer Source ↗ POTA

Nils DF7CW is QRV 16–27 August as SM/DF7CW, aiming at six never-activated POTA parks plus a SOTA summit on Sweden's King's Trail. No cell. Spots go Garmin inReach Mini 2 to SOTAmāt, then onto the POTA/SOTA cluster. Trail radio is a Mountain Topper Currahee at 5 W CW.

RI8KR — Ob Bay islands, Kara Sea, 21–28 August

DX-World Source ↗ DX

Vladimir RK8A and Igor UA3EDQ plan four new RRA island groups in the Ob Bay. Three also count for IOTA AS-109, quiet since 2013. 40–10 m, CW, SSB, maybe digital. QSL via RZ3EC and LoTW. Official page at robinsons.ru/ri8kr.

Colombian amateurs in earthquake response; keep emergency frequencies clear

ARRL Source ↗ OPS

After the 10 August 2026 earthquake in Colombia, LCRA and club stations have been restoring repeaters and supporting rescue comms. IARU R2 asks stations to stay off 3985, 7060, 7240, 7275, 14300, and 21360 kHz, plus 146.520 MHz in VHF range of Colombia.

FCC cites GMRS licensee for a linked repeater network

ARRL Source ↗ POLICY

Enforcement Bureau issued a Notice of Violation to the licensee of WQYU407 for internet-linking nearly two dozen GMRS repeaters. Linking is legal in amateur radio; the FCC says it is not in GMRS because the service has only eight shared repeater pairs.

ARRL National Convention with Huntsville Hamfest, 21–23 August

ARRL Source ↗ OPS

Training tracks and a National Convention dinner at the U.S. Space & Rocket Center on Friday; Huntsville Hamfest Saturday–Sunday at the Von Braun Center, including W1AW America250.

HRWB 269 — AllStarLink with Jason McCormick, N8EI

Ham Radio Workbench Source ↗ WORKBENCH

George KJ6VU talks with one of the AllStarLink developers about radio-over-IP linking of repeaters and individual stations. Published 12 August 2026.

A50QO — satellite DXpedition to Bhutan, 15–20 August

Ham Radio World Source ↗ DX

Feng BA7LVG and Taka JK2XXK are QRV from Thimphu as A50QO, aimed at QO-100 and LEO satellites, with a possible 70 cm EME attempt. QSL via LoTW.

State of POTA 2026 — W8TAM on the next platform

Q5 Worldwide Ham Radio Source ↗ POTA

POTA co-founder Thom Martin W8TAM talks growth to about 90,000 participants and a public beta of a next-generation POTA platform. Also park boundaries, N-Fers, and keeping the rules simple.
