Model notes

Scope. Conceptual-design sizing for conventional-layout and flying-wing electric fixed-wing aircraft, from 300 g park flyers to the ≈100 kg class. Estimates compare configurations; expect ±15–25 % against a built airframe.

Aerodynamics. Trapezoidal planform math; finite-wing lift correction; component parasite-drag buildup (+15 % interference); induced drag CL²/(πeAR). Tails size from volume coefficients; V-tails use the classic area-sum conversion; flying wings put the neutral point at the wing aerodynamic center, take a ≈15 % CL max penalty for reflex/washout trim, and demand a near-zero section pitching moment.

Propulsion & energy. Electrical → shaft → thrust through your efficiencies; static thrust from actuator-disk theory (FoM 0.7); climb is excess power at the minimum-power speed; pack mass from energy density; endurance from usable capacity at cruise current.

Stability. Tail-volume neutral-point method with downwash and a fuselage correction; T-tails see a reduced downwash gradient; static margin is NP − CG in % MAC.

Mission solver. Scaling heuristics (payload fraction, wing loading by launch, power loading, COTS motor/pack classes) center a two-stage Monte Carlo search; launch methods carry stall and all-up-mass ceilings; infeasible briefs report the binding constraint. Structure mass is held at a heuristic fraction — the weakest number in the loop; refine it from build data.

Not modeled. Wind, control-surface sizing, structural sizing/flutter, prop maps vs advance ratio, trim drag, battery sag, BWB center-body lift carryover. Airfoil polars are low-Reynolds summaries.
